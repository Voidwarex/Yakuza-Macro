Three pieces

How it works

A small relay runs on your server. Your PC quietly checks in with it, and the web panel sends the command. Because your PC only reaches out, it works from behind any router with nothing to open.

01 — SERVER

The relay

A tiny Flask app with its own database, hosted on your server at api.amos.fyi. It holds your devices and the commands you queue.

02 — YOUR PC

The listener

A lightweight Python program runs in the background and asks the relay every few seconds whether there's anything to do.

03 — ANYWHERE

The panel

Sign in from any browser, see which PCs are online, and press Shut down, Restart or Lock. The command lands on the next check-in.
