FAQ

Questions people ask

What do I need to run it?

A Windows 10 or 11 PC. The app opens its dashboard in your normal web browser. For controller mode you also install the free ViGEmBus driver once.

Can I use my account on another PC?

Accounts lock to the first PC they sign in from. If you get a new PC, contact support and we'll reset your hardware lock so you can sign in there.

Does it work with PlayStation controllers?

Yes, when Steam Input or DS4Windows is running. They make the pad appear as an Xbox controller, which is what Amos reads. Xbox controllers work as they are.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no keys, subscriptions or paid tiers. You only need a free account, which keeps your settings and your PC lock tied to you.

Why do I need an account?

Accounts let us block abuse and make sure you're running the real, current version of the app. We only ask for a username and password.

Is it safe to use in my game?

Amos sends ordinary key and controller presses, the same as you pressing them. Some games don't allow macros in their rules, so check the rules of any game you play before using it.
