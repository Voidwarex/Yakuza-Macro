How it works

Three small pieces that just work together. Because your computer only reaches out, there's nothing to open on your router.

1

Set it up once

A small, secure service runs on your own server and keeps track of your computers.

2

Add your computer

Install a lightweight helper on each PC. It quietly checks in and waits for your command.

3

Control from anywhere

Open the dashboard in any browser and power your computer off, restart it, or lock the screen.

See every device

Private & secure

Password-protected, encrypted over HTTPS, with per-device keys you can revoke any time. It runs on your server, not ours.

One tap, done
